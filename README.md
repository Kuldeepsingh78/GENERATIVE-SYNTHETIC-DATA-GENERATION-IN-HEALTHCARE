# GENERATIVE-SYNTHETIC-DATA-GENERATION-IN-HEALTHCARE
The paper highlights how synthetic data preserves the statistical properties of real datasets while ensuring privacy, making it highly useful for training machine learning and deep learning models. It reviews several existing approaches, including medGAN, CycleGAN, and CTGAN, which have shown strong performance in medical imaging disease prediction, and data augmentation tasks.

The main objective of the study is to analyze the effectiveness, reliability, and practical applications of synthetic data in healthcare. It evaluates how well synthetic data can replicate real-world patterns and its usefulness in areas like diagnostics, predictive modeling, and personalized treatment.

Additionally, the paper discusses key challenges such as maintaining data quality, ensuring fairness, and addressing ethical and legal concerns. It also suggests future research directions to improve the accuracy and trustworthiness of synthetic data.

The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed model, a Fully Connected Neural Network (FCNN), achieved high performance with accuracy above 98%, along with strong precision, recall, and F1-score, indicating the effectiveness of synthetic data in real-world applications
